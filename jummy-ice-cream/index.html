ABOUT US

Welcome to Jummy Ice Cream, a place where every scoop made to bring a little extra sweetness to your day.

The name Jummy was inspired by the word “yummy”, reflecting our goal of creating ice cream that is fun, delicious, and enjoyable for everyone. We wanted a name that sounds cheerful, memorable and captures the feeling of enjoying a good scoop of ice cream.

At Jummy Ice Cream, we believe that ice cream is more than just a dessert. It's a simple treat that can brighten your day, bring people together, and create sweet memories.
